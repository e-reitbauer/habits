The styles build one graphic device from a real thing in beekeeping's world (for example the hexagonal comb, a hive's frames, a jar label or seal, a flowering calendar, the apiary's map) and use it in at least three places at different scales (large in the opening, as dividers or edges, and as small marks on lists, buttons or captions). A single faint hexagon pattern in the background alone fails, and so does decoration that would suit any business.

Beyond flat color and boxes, the styles add at least one decorative motif drawn from sailing or the lake (for example contour or chart lines, waves, a tacking zigzag, rope, pennants or wind marks) as a pattern, divider, frame or texture, kept quiet behind text. There is no generic decoration such as dot-grid or graph-paper backgrounds, gradient blobs or orbs, or sparkles.

The styles build one graphic device from a real thing in sailing or the lake's world (for example a nautical chart's depth contours, signal flags, a sail's numbers, a course with its marks, rope, a regatta schedule) and use it in at least three places at different scales (for example large in the opening, as section dividers or edges, and as small marks on lists, buttons or captions). A single faint background pattern alone fails.

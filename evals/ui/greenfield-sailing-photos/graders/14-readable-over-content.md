A header or navigation that is position fixed or sticky has a backing that keeps its text readable over the photos and content scrolling beneath it: an opaque background, a translucent one with backdrop-filter, or a state that becomes backed once content scrolls under it. Text placed over photos sits on a scrim, a calm area or its own surface rather than directly on a busy image. Where there is no fixed or sticky bar and no text over photos, this passes.

The colors are a system: they are defined once as custom properties with roles, the neutrals are tinted rather than pure greys (not only #fff, #000 and #888-style greys), and there is one clearly dominant color family with a rarely used accent. A smooth gradient that blends two different hues states its interpolation space (`in oklab` or `in oklch`); hard-stop patterns (stripes, dots, grids) and gradients within one hue don't need one.

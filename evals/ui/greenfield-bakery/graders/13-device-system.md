The styles build one graphic device from a real thing in a bakery's world (for example the scoring cut on a loaf, a proofing basket's rings, a flour sack's print, a bread label or ticket, the oven's schedule) and use it in at least three places at different scales (for example large in the opening, as section dividers or edges, and as small marks on lists, buttons or captions). A single faint background pattern alone, or decoration that would suit any business, fails.

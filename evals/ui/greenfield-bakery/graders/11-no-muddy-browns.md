Text, borders, lines and icons don't use dull dark browns or olives (dark, low-saturation yellows or oranges such as #6e5a3a or #4a3f2a); body text sits in a clean neutral ink. Where the page uses brown because the subject is bread, it is a rich, warm, saturated brown used on purpose, not a muddy one.

The 3D builds at least three distinct settings of the journey from geometry that represents them: for example a train (carriages with windows, a locomotive), terrain or mountains with a viaduct or track, the inside of a sleeper compartment, a station platform, or the lagoon with water and buildings. It is not one object on a turntable, and not generic particles, starfields, torus knots or abstract floating shapes.
